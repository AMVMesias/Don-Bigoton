# Propuestas de gatitos para los márgenes

Generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes de Codex (modo built-in) el 26 de octubre de 2026. Son propuestas visuales estáticas para elegir antes de implementar. Las maquetas de páginas de la imagen son referencias de composición; no sustituyen el contenido de la revista.

Archivo: `previews/propuestas-gatitos-margenes.png`.

- A: gato recostado sobre el borde inferior; cola y parpadeo.
- B: gato asomado al encabezado con las patas delanteras sobre el borde; orejas y aparición.
- C: gato detrás de un sello con hamburguesa; guiño y movimiento de cola.

Referencia visual: logo original de Don Bigotón. Se implementaron las variantes A y B, aprobadas por el usuario, como elementos pequeños de los márgenes. La variante C con hamburguesa se descartó. El logo original se conserva. Los PNG fuente están en `material/creativo/gato-inferior.png` y `gato-encabezado.png`; la animación por capas está en `src/components/MarginCat.astro`.

## Prompt final

+Use case: illustration-story / design exploration.
Create a polished landscape presentation board with THREE distinct proposals for a TINY decorative cat mascot integrated into the margin of an elegant digital magazine. This is a STATIC comparison image for a client to choose a design before implementation.
Reference image 1 is ONLY the existing Don Bigoton brand logo as a character/color reference, NOT an edit target. Keep its friendly orange cat, warm dark brown outlines, cream-white muzzle and gently happy expression. Do not reproduce or redesign its circular logo.
Visual style: refined clean 2D vector-like illustration, smooth organic shapes, warm burnt orange and espresso brown outlines, subtle cream details, elegant editorial quality, charming but not childish. Anatomy must be carefully correct: two front legs and two hind legs naturally placed or plausibly hidden, simple rounded cat paws with no human fingers, no extra legs or disconnected paws. Natural shoulders and elbows. Tails attached correctly. No walking poses, no contorted skeleton, no uncanny limbs.
Board background warm ivory #FFF7E9. Three equal generous columns with thin brown dividers. Each column shows a LARGE clear close-up of its mascot at the top, and a smaller contextual mini magazine page mockup below demonstrating that the mascot occupies only about 8–12% of the page width. Context mini page uses warm ivory paper, fine brown header/footer rules, small understated serif title 'Don Bigotón', tasteful orange accent and abstract gray text lines; the magazine content remains dominant.
Proposal A, label 'A · EN EL BORDE INFERIOR': orange cat comfortably lying along the bottom-right footer rule, two relaxed front paws resting naturally along the line, hind legs tucked under its body, gently curved tail extending to the right, head slightly upright, sleepy happy expression. The entire mascot sits on the rule like a ledge, not in a big panel.
Proposal B, label 'B · ASOMADO AL ENCABEZADO': cat peeks from behind the TOP-RIGHT paper edge with head and two front paws clearly visible resting over the top rule. Its body and back legs are plausibly hidden behind the paper. Face engaging and friendly, ears symmetrical with slight character, tail tip visible to the side. No floating head; the two paws grip the edge naturally.
Proposal C, label 'C · CON UN SELLO': small cat is tucked behind a small circular cream editorial badge bearing a simple orange hamburger pictogram. Two front paws rest naturally over the top edge of the badge, hindquarters concealed behind it, tail curls around the badge. Position this tiny badge-cat at bottom-right of the miniature magazine page.
Put a small concise motion note under each large close-up: A 'Cola suave + parpadeo'; B 'Se asoma + mueve las orejas'; C 'Abraza el sello + guiña'.
Top board title exact: 'Gatitos para acompañar la lectura'.
All lettering legible and tasteful, all artwork fully visible with ample spacing, no clipped characters. No large central cat scenes in the magazine mockups, no photo of burgers, no cartoon backgrounds. This is a professional visual proposal board, not a finished website or animated GIF.
